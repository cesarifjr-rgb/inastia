### real-estate

Create an exquisite hand-painted gouache illustration, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, full bleed, no border. A refined, restrained illustrated travel-journal style with visible painterly brushwork, subtle paper grain, luminous Mediterranean afternoon light, delicate olive-leaf shadows. Palette: warm ivory, honey limestone, sage green, terracotta, muted marine blue. A clear and balanced composition that reads beautifully at 350px wide. Keep the main objects within the central 85 percent and separate them clearly. Clearly an illustration, not a photograph. No people, hands, logos, signs, text, lettering, watermarks, frame or UI. Subject: a beautifully arranged stone writing desk on a Corsican terrace. In the foreground, a pair of brass house keys on a sage linen ribbon rests beside a closed ivory property folder, a simple unlettered architectural floor plan and one elegant navy pencil. The keys are the clear focal point. A small olive branch rests nearby. In the softly painted background, a glimpse of a pale stone house with sage shutters and the blue sea. Convey thoughtful continuity and care for a homeowner's property project. No signatures, contract wording, money or handover scene.
